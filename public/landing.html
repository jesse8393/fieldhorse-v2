One pipeline, five stages

Lead, quote, job, invoice, closed. Drag a card forward and Fieldhorse handles the paperwork behind it. Nothing falls through the cracks between a phone call and a contract.

Crew schedule that holds up

Assign crews by the day, see conflicts before they happen, and shuffle a rain delay across the week in two taps. Everyone gets the update on their phone.

AI proposals from a voice note

Walk the site, talk through the scope, and Fieldhorse drafts a line-itemed proposal in your voice with your pricing. Review, tweak, send before you leave the driveway.

How it works

Ten minutes to your first quote

No onboarding calls. No implementation fees. It's a phone app for people who build things.

Add your jobs

Import contacts or just type the three jobs on your mind. Each one lands in the pipeline at the right stage, with the client, address, and numbers attached.

Send a proposal from the truck. When it's signed, the job slides forward, crew days hit the calendar, and the deposit invoice goes out automatically.
