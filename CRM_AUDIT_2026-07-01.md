# Fieldhorse v2 — CRM Scaling & Ease-of-Use Audit

**Date:** July 1, 2026
**Branch/commit audited:** `main` @ `672fdf3` (delta since the June 11 audit at `3287d19` — 12 commits, ~15k lines: client money tools, statements & pay links, change-order e-sign, push, webhook lead capture, pipeline v2, universal capture, offline outbox).
**Method:** clean-tree baseline (typecheck / lint / 110 unit tests / build / `npm audit` all green) plus six parallel deep-review passes — backend edge-function security, edge-function *logic*, database/RLS, frontend data layer, UX/ease-of-use, and money-path correctness. Every serverless function and migration was read in full; every headline finding below was re-verified by hand against the source and the generated schema types.

> **Note on the green baseline.** The passing test suite is misleading for two of the most serious findings: `partner-invite-accept` and `webhook-lead` both `INSERT`/`SELECT` columns that do not exist in the schema, yet no test exercises those inserts — so CI is green while both features are broken at runtime. Tests pass ≠ features work.

---

## 1. Executive summary

The tree is **healthy on the surface**: typecheck, lint, 110 unit tests, and the production build all pass; `npm audit` reports **0 vulnerabilities** (the June-11 residual 6 are gone after the jspdf 2→4 upgrade); vendor code-splitting — a prior deduction — is now in place. The June-11 critical fixes (auth on all `send-*` functions and the `/api/claude` proxy caps) are **intact and verified**.

But the ~15k lines added since June 11 introduced regressions concentrated in the four areas the user cares about most — **tenant isolation, two outright-broken features, change-order money accounting, and scaling**:

1. **CRITICAL — cross-tenant data via mintable public links.** The `fh_public_links` RLS policy anchors only `org_id`, not the row's `user_id`/`contact_id`/`client_id`, and the public-link function trusts `link.user_id` as the tenant scope. An accepted partner (who knows the inviter's `user_id`) can mint a token that serves the victim's full job/payment/statement payload — and can flip a victim's change order to "approved."
2. **CRITICAL — invoice PDFs over-bill the customer by the change-order amount.** `InvoiceDetail` now passes a CO-inclusive `contractTotal` *and* the change-order list into `generateInvoice`, which adds the approved COs a **second** time. Approved change orders are accounted three different ways across the invoice surfaces (correct on-screen, double-counted in the emitted/emailed PDF, ignored entirely on the public pay link — the last shows "PAID" while money is owed).
3. **CRITICAL — two shipped features are broken by column mismatches that tests don't cover.** `partner-invite-accept` selects a non-existent `fh_job_partners.user_id` (every acceptance 500s), and `webhook-lead` inserts a non-existent `fh_contacts.source` (every inbound lead fails). Both were added/changed in this range; both pass CI because no test hits the insert.
4. **HIGH — the "crew labor in margin" headline feature costs $0.** Nothing ever writes `hourly_rate` on crew punches, so every crew hour lands as "unrated" and contributes nothing to job cost/margin.
5. **HIGH — the scaling ceiling is a family of fetch-everything queries.** Jobs, Leads, Quotes, Pipeline, Clients, Invoices, and Analytics all run unbounded `select('*')` on ever-growing tables, filter in JS, and — via realtime — refetch the whole payload on any change. This also silently breaks offline persistence (localStorage quota) at a few thousand rows.
6. **HIGH — more money-integrity gaps:** `partner-invite` has no authentication at all (reopens the white-label email-relay hole for that one endpoint), payment writes aren't idempotent (lost-response retap → duplicate payment → wrong auto-close), and auto-close ignores approved change orders.

**Health score: 64/100.** The data-model, RLS foundation, and test discipline are genuinely good, but this delta shipped a tenant-isolation break, two runtime-broken features that CI can't see, and customer-facing over/under-billing on invoices — all in the money features that were the point of the release. Encouragingly, nearly every top item is a **small diff** (a policy clause, an auth check, a column name, one arithmetic owner).

Full detail below. Severity key: **CRITICAL** (tenant/security boundary or data loss), **HIGH** (feature broken or scaling ceiling), **MEDIUM/LOW** (correctness/polish).

## 2. Security & tenant isolation

### CRITICAL

**S1 — Mintable cross-tenant public links (RLS gap + service-role trust).**
`supabase/migrations/034_crew_portal_phase_2_fixed.sql:220-222` — the `fh_public_links_own` policy is a single `FOR ALL` with `WITH CHECK (org_id IN (select auth_user_org_ids()))`. It validates **only** `org_id`; the row's `user_id`, `contact_id`, `client_id`, `change_order_id` are caller-supplied and unchecked. `netlify/functions/public-link.js:127,290` then resolves tenancy as `.eq('user_id', link.user_id)` with a **service-role** client (RLS bypassed).
*Exploit:* an accepted partner knows the inviter's `user_id` and a `job_id` from their own `fh_job_partners` row. They `INSERT` a `fh_public_links` row with `user_id = victim`, `contact_id = victim's job`, and their own `org_id` (which passes `WITH CHECK`). Opening `/p/:token` serves the victim's full job payload — all payments (there is no partner-read policy on `fh_payments`), invoices, insurance, quote items, signed photo URLs. With `kind='statement'` + a `client_id`, the client's entire A/R. With `kind='change_order'`, `public-co-approve.js` will flip the victim's CO to `approved` with a typed signature.
*Fix:* split `fh_public_links` into per-command policies; `INSERT ... WITH CHECK` must require `user_id = (select auth.uid())` **and** an `EXISTS` proving `contact_id`/`client_id`/`change_order_id` belong to the same org. Defense-in-depth: in the functions, derive tenancy from `contact.org_id = link.org_id` rather than trusting `link.user_id`.

**S1b — Two shipped features are broken by column mismatches (schema ≠ code).**
- `netlify/functions/partner-invite-accept.js:68,100` — the invite lookup selects `id, job_id, partner_email, status, user_id`, but `fh_job_partners` has no `user_id` column (only `invited_by_user_id`/`partner_user_id` — verified in migration `004_partner_jobs.sql:15-16` and the generated types). The `user_id` select was added in this range to feed a new `sendPushToUser(admin, invite.user_id, …)`. Result: **every** valid invite click → PostgREST 42703 → 500 `lookup_failed`; no partner can ever be accepted. *Fix:* select and push to `invited_by_user_id`.
- `netlify/functions/webhook-lead.js:123` — the insert includes `source: …`, but `fh_contacts` has no `source` column (the `source` field in the generated types belongs to `fh_materials`; verified). Every inbound webhook lead → PGRST204 → 502; the provider retries and the rate-limiter counts the retries, but zero leads ever land. *(Caveat: this key may predate the range — but no migration adds the column, so it is broken against the repo schema as it stands.)* *Fix:* drop the `source` key or add the column in a migration and a test covering the insert.

These are CRITICAL in impact (feature completely non-functional) but trivial diffs. They matter especially because **the green test suite hides them** — no test exercises either insert.

### HIGH

**S2 — `partner-invite.js` has no caller authentication.**
`netlify/functions/partner-invite.js` never calls `auth.getUser()` (verified: 0 occurrences). Ownership is checked as `fh_contacts.id == job_id AND user_id == invited_by_user_id`, both taken from the request body. Anyone who supplies a valid `(job_id, owner_user_id)` pair can send white-labeled partner-invite email through the contractor's Resend sender and mint `fh_job_partners` invite tokens — the exact open-relay class the June-11 pass fixed on the other `send-*` functions but missed here.
*Fix:* add the same bearer-token check used by `docusign-send.js`/`send-quote.js` — verify `auth.getUser(token).id === invited_by_user_id` before the ownership query.

**S3 — Crew can self-approve / edit their own time punches.**
`supabase/migrations/036_fh_time_punches.sql:97-100` — `fh_time_punches_self` is `FOR ALL` with `USING/WITH CHECK (user_id = auth.uid())`. RLS is row-level, so a crew member can PostgREST-`update` their own rows' `approved_at`, `approved_by`, `hourly_rate`, `punch_in_at`, and clear `flagged` directly, bypassing the manager-role re-check in `org-punch-approve.js`. Falsified hours/rates then flow into job margin and payroll (`src/lib/labor.ts`).
*Fix:* a `BEFORE UPDATE` trigger rejecting changes to `approved_*`/`flagged`/`hourly_rate` (and `punch_in_at` after approval) unless the writer is the service role.

**S4 — DocuSign webhook is fail-open on HMAC.**
`netlify/functions/docusign-webhook.js:49-75` — with `DOCUSIGN_CONNECT_HMAC_KEY` unset (the default), unsigned events are accepted with a `console.warn`. An attacker who knows any `envelopeId` can POST a forged `status:'completed'` and flip `fh_contacts.proposal_status='approved'`. The timing-safe compare is correct *when* a key is set — the gap is the default.
*Fix:* fail-closed once Connect HMAC is provisioned; reject "envelope exists in our table but event unsigned."

**S5 — No rate limiting on public token / AI endpoints.**
Only `webhook-lead.js` has a durable limiter (migration 052). `public-link.js`, `public-link-approve.js`, `public-co-approve.js`, and the invite-info endpoints have none — each miss still costs a full service-role round trip, and a valid link's approve path lets anyone spray notification/DB writes. `claude.js` requires a JWT and caps `max_tokens`/messages (June-11 fix intact) but has **no per-user spend cap and no `model` allow-list**, so any signed-in user can loop 8k-token calls on an arbitrary (e.g. Opus-class) model against the shared key.
*Fix:* reuse the `fh_increment_webhook_rate_limit` RPC pattern per-IP/per-token across public endpoints; add a per-user token budget + `model` allow-list to `claude.js`.

**S6 — `public-link-approve.js` returns `ok:true` when the contact update fails — half-applied, re-approvable.**
`netlify/functions/public-link-approve.js:231-240` — after inserting the approved `fh_quote_versions` row, a failed `fh_contacts` update (`proposal_status='approved'`) is only `console.error`'d and the function still returns 200. The customer sees "approved" but `proposal_status` stays `sent`, so the idempotency gate (which reads `proposal_status`) no longer blocks — a refresh + re-sign inserts a second approved version, overwriting the recorded signature/timestamp. *Fix:* check the error and return 500, or do the insert+update in one RPC. (Structure predates the range; flagged as in-scope.)

### MEDIUM / LOW (security)

- **Email-match identity without a verified-email gate** (`sub-profile-update.js`, `sub-doc-confirm.js`, `sub-portal-context.js`): these authenticate the caller and match the sub by `ilike('email', authEmail)` but never check `email_confirmed_at` (verified: not referenced anywhere in the functions). **If** the Supabase project doesn't strictly enforce email confirmation at signup, an attacker can register a known sub's email and read/overwrite their EIN/insurance/payment data. Severity is conditional on project auth config — **verify the setting**, and gate on `email_confirmed_at` regardless. (M/High)
- **Statement/proposal links never expire** — `src/lib/publicLink.ts` defaults `expires_at = null`; combined with S1 and no rate limit, a forwarded statement link exposes a client's whole financial relationship forever. Default a 30–90 day expiry. (Medium)
- **`From` display-name injection** — `company_name` is interpolated unescaped into the RFC5322 `From` header across `send-*`/`partner-invite`. Sanitize CR/LF/`"`/`<`/`>`. (Medium)
- **`org-members-list.js`/`org-timesheets-list.js`** call `admin.auth.admin.listUsers({perPage:1000})` — an O(all-platform-users) call behind an O(org) feature that silently drops emails past 1000 total users. Use `getUserById` per member or store email on `profiles`. (Medium, also a scaling bug)
- **`webhook-lead` key in URL query string** (log exposure) — accept via header instead. (Medium)
- **`delete-account.js`** purges a hand-maintained table list that omits newer tables (sub profiles, public links, punches…) — GDPR/App-Store gap; drive off FK `ON DELETE CASCADE` from `auth.users`. (Low)
- Raw Supabase `error.message` returned to clients in several functions (recon aid); negative `amount` accepted in `webhook-lead`. (Low)

### Verified intact / healthy

June-11 fixes confirmed present: all five `send-*` functions + `docusign-send` verify a Supabase JWT **and** enforce `user.id === sender_user_id`; `claude.js` requires a JWT, caps `max_tokens` at 8192 and messages at 50, and defaults to `claude-sonnet-4-6`. Org-admin endpoints implement a correct role-tier ladder with no cross-org IDOR, block last-owner removal and privilege escalation. `webhook-lead.js` is the model public endpoint (atomic rate limit, 64KB cap, server-resolved org). Token entropy is sound (~137-bit link tokens via `crypto.getRandomValues`; `gen_random_bytes` invite tokens). Email **body** escaping and pay-URL scheme allow-listing are consistent. New tables (`fh_push_subscriptions`, `fh_app_config`, `fh_webhook_rate_limits`, `fh_public_link_events`) have correct least-privilege RLS; the 052 SECURITY DEFINER RPCs pin `search_path` and revoke from public/anon/authenticated.

## 3. Scaling

The dominant scaling risk is **fetch-everything-then-filter-in-JS**, corroborated independently by the frontend and database passes.

### HIGH

**SC1 — Unbounded `select('*')` powers every list surface.**
`src/lib/queries.ts:52-55` (`fetchJobs`) selects `*, fh_clients(...)` with an `order` but **no `.limit()` and no server-side stage filter** (verified). It backs Jobs, Leads, Quotes, and Pipeline, each of which filters to its stage slice **in JS**. `fh_contacts` is a wide row with `photos`/`milestones` jsonb; at 5,000 jobs this is a multi-MB payload downloaded to render 40 leads.
*Fix:* per-surface queries with `.in('stage', [...])`, a narrow projection (cards use ~10 fields), `.limit()` + keyset pagination on `updated_at`.

**SC2 — Analytics fetches seven whole tables and aggregates in JS.**
`src/lib/queries.ts:616-631` (`fetchAnalyticsBundle`) pulls every contact, payment, invoice, change order, and mileage row — no limits — then computes every KPI client-side, cached 24h and pushed into the persister. Tens of MB at a multi-year org.
*Fix:* Postgres views/RPCs returning KPI aggregates; date-window the raw rows the charts actually plot.

**SC3 — Invoices/AR and Clients bundles are unbounded too.**
`fetchInvoicesBundle` (`queries.ts:312-336`) and `fetchClientsBundle` (`queries.ts:120-136`) select all payments + all invoices/contacts to compute aging and A/R in JS, ignoring the trigger-maintained `fh_clients.total_lifetime_value`/`active_jobs_count` columns that already exist. Payments grow forever.

**SC4 — Realtime × fetch-everything = refetch storm.**
`useJobsRealtime` (`queries.ts:87-100`) invalidates `['jobs']` — the full SC1 payload — on **any** `fh_contacts` change; `useHomeDashboardRealtime` (`homeDashboard.ts:619-645`) fires 8 parallel queries (incl. re-signing photo URLs) on wildcard events across **7 tables**, undebounced. A 5-person org editing jobs every ~30s continuously re-downloads all contacts — a client-side self-DDoS on Supabase egress.
*Fix:* debounce invalidations (trailing 3–5s) or apply the changed row surgically with `setQueryData`.

**SC5 — Offline persistence silently dies at the scale it exists for.**
`src/main.tsx` persists the whole query cache (containing the multi-MB SC1–SC3 snapshots) to **localStorage** (~5 MB quota). Past ~2–3k jobs `setItem` throws and persistence stops with no signal — the offline cold-open feature disappears exactly when a real account reaches it; the sync `JSON.stringify` of a multi-MB cache also janks the main thread.
*Fix:* move to an IndexedDB async persister and `shouldDehydrateQuery`-whitelist only the slim queries. (The `buster`=build-SHA cache versioning is already correct.)

**SC6 — No list virtualization.**
Jobs/Clients/Leads/Invoices render unbounded arrays of framer-motion `SwipeableRow` components (`Jobs.tsx:539`, `Clients.tsx:462`, …) with no `react-window`/`react-virtual` anywhere; search filters re-render the full list per keystroke with no `useDeferredValue`. 5,000 job rows = multi-second commit + keystroke lag.
*Fix:* virtualize, or incremental "show more" + defer search.

**SC7 — Cover-photo pipeline fetches every photo row and re-signs on a 30s-stale dashboard.**
`src/lib/photos.ts:22-27` pulls every `fh_job_files` photo row to keep the newest per job, inside `fetchHomeDashboard` (staleTime 30s + refetch-on-focus + every SC4 invalidation). 5k jobs × 20 photos = 100k rows fetched to show ~6 images.

### MEDIUM (scaling)

- **Org-mode scoping is inconsistent** — Subs/Home were converted to `org_id` scoping, but Clients/Invoices/Analytics/Activity/Notes/universalSearch and `useJobsRealtime` still filter `user_id`, so in a multi-member org an admin sees $0 of a teammate's invoices and Jobs never live-refreshes on a teammate's edit. (`queries.ts:125-135,255-269,313-335`; `universalSearch.ts:77-109`)
- **Missing indexes / retention:** no `pg_trgm`/FTS index for the leading-wildcard universal search (`universalSearch.ts:73-113`); no index on `fh_public_links.change_order_id`; `fh_webhook_rate_limits` and `fh_public_link_events` are append-only with no TTL (~1,440 rows/day/key forever). Add trigram GIN, the partial FK index, and a pg_cron retention job.
- **jsPDF loaded eagerly** on `/invoices` and `/clients/:id` — `statement.ts`/`invoices.ts` statically import `pdf.js`, defeating the lazy `loadPdf()` elsewhere. Make those two `await import('./pdf.js')` internally (~430KB off those routes).
- **`putEntry` loads the whole outbox** (photo Blobs included) just to enforce the cap (`outbox.ts:89-95`) — use `store.count()`.

### Verified OK (scaling)

Routes are lazy-loaded with sensible `manualChunks`; recharts is confined to lazy Analytics. Realtime channels are all cleaned up and filtered; `enabled` guards are consistent; global `staleTime` is 30s (no `staleTime:0` heavy refetch). The main **insert** outbox is genuinely idempotent (client UUID + `upsert onConflict:id ignoreDuplicates`, single-flight, oldest-first). Bounded queries exist where it matters (activity 60/type, notes 80, notifications 40, punches 14). `org_id` is indexed on all 28 tenant tables and `auth.uid()` is initplan-wrapped in policies.

## 4. Money-path correctness

### CRITICAL — approved change orders are accounted three different ways across the invoice surfaces

This is the single most damaging cluster in the release: the same job's change-order money is over-billed on the emitted PDF, correct on-screen, and ignored on the public pay link.

**M-0a — Invoice PDF double-counts approved COs → customer over-billed.**
`src/screens/InvoiceDetail.tsx:137-139` computes `totals.amount = contact.amount + approvedCO`, then `:207,210` passes `contractTotal: totals.amount` **and** `changeOrders` into `generateInvoice`, which recomputes `ct = contractTotal + approvedCO` (`src/lib/pdf.js:834-837`). The approved COs are added twice. Same defect in the new `buildInvoicePdf` (`src/lib/invoices.ts:119,147`) and via `InvoiceTemplate` (`components/documents/InvoiceTemplate.tsx:51-54`).
*Example:* contract $10,000, approved CO +$2,000, paid $6,000. The emailed/downloaded invoice shows **Contract total $14,000** and **BALANCE REMAINING $8,000** — the customer is over-billed by **$2,000** (true balance $6,000). Correct at the June-11 base, where `contractTotal` was the raw `contact.amount`.
*Fix:* pick one owner for the CO adjustment — pass raw `contact.amount` as `contractTotal` (the PDF/template self-adjust), or stop re-adding inside them.

**M-0b — Public invoice link ignores COs → shows "PAID" while money is owed.**
`src/screens/PublicDoc.tsx:353-354,376` — `InvoiceView` sets `contractTotal = contact.amount` (no COs), `balance = max(0, contractTotal - paid)`, and `status = balance < 0.5 ? 'paid' : 'outstanding'`, feeding the `PayNowBar` amount — while the `InvoiceTemplate` body on the same page displays the CO-adjusted total, so the page contradicts itself.
*Example:* contract $10,000, customer e-signs a +$2,000 CO through the new public flow ("It's on the next invoice automatically"), pays $10,000. The public link then shows **PAID**, hides the Pay-now bar, and reports **balance $0** while **$2,000** is still owed — defeating the CO e-sign feature shipped in the same range.
*Fix:* add the approved-CO sum to `contractTotal` in `InvoiceView`, mirroring `contractTotals()`.

Net effect: a contractor who uses change orders cannot trust any invoice number, and the customer-facing document (the PDF) is the one that's wrong in the contractor's favor — the worst place for the error to live. **A single shared `contractTotals(contact, changeOrders)` helper should be the only place COs are folded in, consumed by on-screen, PDF, and public views alike.**

### HIGH

**M-1 — "Crew labor in job cost + margin" contributes $0.**
`src/lib/timePunches.ts` `punchIn`/`punchOut` accept an optional `hourlyRate`, but the crew callers (`Crew.tsx:157,177`) pass none, and `crewLaborForContact` (`labor.ts:41-66`) deliberately excludes the owner's punches (the only ones that carry a rate). So every counted crew punch has `hourly_rate = null` → files as `unratedHours`, `cost += 0`. A crew member working 40h adds **$0** to job cost; margin shows all crew labor as free. (Verified end-to-end.)
*Fix:* resolve the member's rate from an org member-rate source on punch-out (or in `recalcCost`) and stamp `hourly_rate`.

**M-2 — Payment writes aren't idempotent → duplicate payments, wrong auto-close.**
`src/lib/stages.ts:163` `logPayment` inserts `fh_payments` with no client-generated id or upsert. On a jobsite where the request commits but the response is lost, the UI shows an error, the operator retaps, and a **duplicate payment row** lands; the inflated total then auto-closes the job (`stages.ts:219`). Capture-flow expenses/notes survive dead zones via the outbox, but a captured *payment* just throws offline — the highest-stakes write is the least resilient.
*Fix:* mint `id = crypto.randomUUID()` client-side + `upsert(onConflict:'id', ignoreDuplicates)`; keep money out of the delayed-replay outbox but make the immediate path idempotent and show an explicit "not saved — offline" state.

**M-3 — Auto-close ignores approved change orders.**
`src/lib/stages.ts:212-218` compares total payments against `contact.amount` only, while `contractTotals`/`gatherStatement`/Invoices all use `amount + approved COs`. Job $10,000 + approved CO $2,000, client pays $10,000 → job flips to `closed` and the remaining $2,000 owed silently drops out of statements and A/R.
*Fix:* fold approved COs into `contractAmount` before the auto-close comparison.

### MEDIUM

- **Divergent "outstanding" totals on the same screen** — `ClientDetail.tsx:83-96` computes `outstanding` as `amount - paid` (no COs), while the statement button on the same screen (line 106) includes COs; the two tiles disagree for the same client. Route `outstanding` through the CO-aware `gatherStatement`.
- **Duplicate draft invoices on retried "Email balance"** — `Invoices.tsx:315-354` calls `createInvoice` before send with no dedupe; a failed send leaves an orphan draft, and repeat taps accumulate "Balance due" rows that inflate `contractTotals.invoiced` and progress-draw numbering. Reuse an existing open draft.
- **"Completed" is honored in 2 of ~5 money surfaces** — `completeJob` now sets `completed_at` but leaves stage=`job`; Home/Analytics account for it, but `rollups.ts`/`statement.ts` still bucket a completed-unpaid job as fully active pipeline. Add one shared `isAwaitingPayment(contact)` helper.

### Verified clean (money)

`gatherStatement` CO-folding + `<= 0.5` epsilon (matches `statement.test.ts`); `payLink.safePayUrl` scheme allow-list (client == server behavior); the **jsPDF 2→4 upgrade is API-correct** for 4.2.1 + autotable 5.0.8 (all 6 smoke tests pass); `contractTotals` approved-CO/void handling; currency arithmetic (no float-equality on money, no cents/dollars mismatch client↔`send-*`); date anchoring (noon-local for due dates, UTC-diff punch hours are DST-correct); `computeFunnel` distinct-contact logic.

## 5. Ease of use

The app's core ergonomics are genuinely strong (see "does well" below); the friction is in redundancy from the recent workflow splits and a few broken promises.

### HIGH

**U-1 — The demo-data exit is a broken promise (and a data-loss trap).**
Onboarding tells users "Sample data… Wipe anytime in Settings" (`Onboarding.tsx:419`), but the wipe control is gated `DEV_BUILD || email === 'test@test.com'` (`Settings.tsx:90`) — a real contractor **cannot remove it**. Worse, `demoSeed.ts` writes **untagged** rows (no `is_demo` flag — verified) and `wipeTestData` deletes **all** of the user's rows, so once real work mixes in, even the dev wipe is destructive.
*Fix:* tag seeded rows with a batch id/`source:'demo'`, expose "Remove sample data" to everyone, and scope the delete to tagged rows only.

**U-2 — Only Universal Capture survives offline; every other form fails blocking.**
NewLeadSheet, V3PaymentSheet, AddEventSheet, LogMilesSheet, NewClientSheet all `await` the insert with no outbox fallback — on a dead-zone jobsite the save just fails. The outbox infrastructure exists; the forms don't use it. (This is the same root as M-2 for payments.)

**U-3 — Destructive confirmations split across two systems.**
`useConfirm`/`ConfirmSheet` is used in some flows, but **11 sites still use `window.confirm()`** (Notes, ChangeOrders, InvoiceDraws, Materials, DailyLogs, Insurance, Selections, Team, Bid) — browser-chrome dialogs block the thread and look broken in a PWA. Mechanical swap to `useConfirm`.

### MEDIUM

- **Four overlapping quote entry points** (Quotes desk, Pipeline's create button, Bid/Estimates AI, a lead's Quote tab) — the split meant to answer "where do I go to do X" answers quoting four ways; `PIPELINE_V2.md` already names the convergence. Pipeline also renders desktop chrome (Ctrl-K hint, "records visible") on the mobile More drawer.
- **Money/time actions live below the fold on job detail** — "Send invoice / Log payment / Mark complete" and the time clock render after the cockpit, next-action, progress meter, and full activity feed (`Overview.tsx:315-395`); the two most frequent field actions need a long one-handed scroll. Pin a compact thumb-zone action row.
- **No fast time-punch for owners** — punching requires navigating into a job and scrolling; not in Home QuickActions or Capture.
- **Two sheets lose data if dismissed mid-save** — `NewClientSheet.tsx:78` and `LogMilesSheet.tsx:76` pass close through with no `!saving` guard (the other sheets have it).
- **Silent intel failure on Leads** — proposal view-tracking fetch failure only `console.error`s and empties the map, so "Viewed 2h ago — follow up" (the screen's hottest cue) silently reads "Not viewed yet" forever on a flaky connection.
- **No `autoComplete` on contact fields** anywhere — forfeits OS autofill of customer phone/address (pure typing tax with dirty hands). `inputMode`/`type` coverage is otherwise complete.
- **Pipeline shows false "0 leads · $0" while loading** and has no empty state; new users read it as broken.
- **Sub-44px touch targets** on the high-frequency `FilterPill` (~34px) and DailyLogs icons (~28px).

### Does well (keep)

## 6. Build / deploy integrity

**B-1 (HIGH) — Duplicate migration version `052`.** `052_public_edge_audit_and_rate_limits.sql` and `052_statement_links.sql` share the version prefix (verified). The Supabase CLI keys `schema_migrations` on the numeric version, so `db push`/`migration list` can record only one — a fresh environment or CI branch DB can silently miss one file's DDL (e.g. statement links live in prod but the scope CHECK/rate-limit RPC absent in a new branch). **Rename one to `053_` before provisioning any new environment.**

Other integrity notes: `org_id` remains nullable everywhere (the Phase-2 "flip to NOT NULL" never shipped — NULL-org rows are invisible to every policy); `fh_set_org_id` picks the most-recently-joined org for multi-org users (a sub in two orgs gets rows stamped to the wrong one); no `CHECK (amount >= 0)` on any money column; job delete cascades payment/invoice history (tax-record loss — prefer soft-delete or `ON DELETE RESTRICT` once a payment exists); no unique constraint backing the `findOrCreateClient` assumption (concurrent webhook + capture create duplicate clients).
